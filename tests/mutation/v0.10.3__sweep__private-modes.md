## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness. Before the sweep, a hand-picked red-green audit of 13 named mutants (`tests/one-offs/thinking/redgreen-0-10-3-private-modes/redgreen.py`) also went all red. The two sets overlap in about half their targets.
